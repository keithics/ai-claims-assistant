# Motor claims

- The excess (the part you pay) is **SGD 500 (SGD 1,000 if the driver is under 21)** per claim.
- Take photos of the scene and the damage before moving the vehicle if it is safe.
- In Singapore, a police report must be made within 24 hours for theft.
- Do not authorise repairs before we confirm the claim, unless the vehicle is unsafe to leave.
