# Required documents

For every claim: a completed claim form, a copy of your policy schedule and a valid government photo ID.

- **Motor:** photos of the damage, repair estimate, driver's licence and vehicle registration. In Singapore, a police report must be made within 24 hours for theft.
- **Health:** hospital bill, doctor's report and discharge summary.
- **Home:** photos of the damage, proof of ownership and a repair quote.

Send documents in clear photos or PDFs. Do not include card numbers.
