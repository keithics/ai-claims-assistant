# Claim deadlines

- Report the incident to us within **14 days** of the incident.
- Submit all supporting documents within **30 days** of the incident.
- Approved claims are paid within **10 working days** after we receive complete documents.

Late reports may be declined unless you can show a good reason for the delay.
