# Claim status and speaking to a person

Ask the assistant for the status of a claim using its claim number, for example the number format `PH-1042`. Status updates can take a little time because our claims system is slow at busy hours.

You can ask for a human agent at any time and complaints are handled by the call centre. Claims above the limit shown on your schedule are always reviewed by a person.
