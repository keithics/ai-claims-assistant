# How to file a claim

Kalayaan Mutual Philippines handles motor, health and home claims. Follow these steps.

1. Report the incident within **30 days** of the date it happened.
2. Gather the documents listed in the required documents guide.
3. Submit your claim through the assistant, the app or the call centre.
4. A claim draft is prepared for you. You must review and approve it before it is filed. The assistant never files a claim by itself.
5. After all documents are received we aim to pay within **15 working days**.

You are talking to an AI assistant. You can ask for a human agent at any time.
