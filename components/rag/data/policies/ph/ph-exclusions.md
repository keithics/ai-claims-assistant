# What is not covered

The following are not covered in the Philippines:

- Damage from driving through floodwater deeper than the wheel hub.
- Losses while the vehicle is used in racing or speed contests.
- Accidents where the driver has no valid Philippine licence.

Check your policy wording for the full list.
